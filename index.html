01  /  Profile

About

I currently serve as a Certificate Peshkar with the Government of Bangladesh in Manikganj District — a role built on preparing, verifying, and safeguarding official certificates and public land records with precision and accountability.

Over more than nine years in grassroots-level government administration, I've seen first-hand where the land record system serves people well, and where it doesn't. That experience is what led me back to the classroom: I'm now pursuing a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) at Khondokar Nurul Hossain Law Academy, focused on Land Law, statutory compliance, and reducing corruption in land registration.

My work sits at the intersection of two things I take seriously — disciplined public administration and a belief that legal reform, backed by real field experience, can make land governance in Bangladesh fairer and more transparent.
